**Lecture Scenario:** The Smiths and the Walkers are neighbors and they do not get along with each other. The Smiths like to have parties at their home that often get very loud. The Walkers are tired of the

noise and confront the Smiths. The Smiths and Walkers begin calling each other names and threatening physical violence against one another. A third neighbor hears the commotion and calls police. Police meet with both families. The Walkers inform the police of the constant noise from the Smiths and tell police they often see small children at these parties until the early morning hours and all the adults are drunk, leaving the children unsupervised.
- Student must be able to articulate the duties of the officers responding to the call.
- Students must know what information the officers are required to report in this scenario.

**Lecture Scenario:** Officers are responding to a call regarding a small group of students causing a riot outside of a high school. When the officers arrive, they find a group of 10-15 students rioting. There are teachers and campus security also present outside the school, however, they are not taking any action to stop the students.
- Students will discuss what actions should be taken at this point and what powers they are able to exercise to stop the riot. Are they authorized to summon aid if needed?
- What is the agency’s policy, if any, on dealing with student riots at schools? Are they handled the same as any other riot?

**Lecture Scenario:** Officers are responding to a call regarding a family disturbance. The officers meet with the caller who informs the officer that her estranged husband is inside her home and is refusing to leave until she agrees to reconcile with him. She tells officers she has a protective order against him due to him physically abusing her. The officers meet with the subject who is inside the home. He tells officers that he was not aware of the order; therefore, there is nothing they can do to him. The officers learn that the order issued is a valid temporary ex-parte order.
- Students will discuss if the officers are able to enforce the order due to the subject having no knowledge of it.
- Students will be able to articulate what the order should state for a violation to be taking place in this scenario.
- Students must be able to explain the steps to obtain a protective order.

**Instructor Note:** Instructors should emphasize that the conduct must be prohibited by the protective order or there is no violation.
