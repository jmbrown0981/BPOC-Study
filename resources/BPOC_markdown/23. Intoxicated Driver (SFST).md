# Chapter 23: Intoxicated Driver (SFST)

**Unit Goal:** Demonstrate and apply a working knowledge of the detection, apprehension, and arrest of the intoxicated driver.
## 23.1 Successful completion of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) SFST Course.
**Instructor Note:** Follow objectives of the newest SFST course. See Abstract for website to find the most current materials.
**Instructor Note:** It is highly encouraged to conduct at least one "wet lab workshop" (with actual drinking subjects).

**Lecture Scenario:** Discuss with students.
- STATUTORY AUTHORIZATION – MANDATORY BLOOD SPECIMEN FORM
- Curtis v. State - Texas Court of Criminal Appeals: Reference reasonable suspicion to make traffic stop based on a vehicle weaving. The training and experience of the officer, most specifically as it applies to DWI enforcement, was taken into account in this case and it was held that the stop for fail to maintain a single marked lane was reasonable.
