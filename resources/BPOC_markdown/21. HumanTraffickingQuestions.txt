# LO 21.2
What is the definition of TRAFFICKING according to the Palermo Protocol?	The recruitment, transportation, transfer, harboring or receipt of persons, by means of the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, of abduction, of fraud, of deception, of the abuse of power or of a position of vulnerability or of the giving or receiving of payments or benefits to achieve the consent of a person having control over another person, for the purpose of exploitation. Exploitation shall include, at a minimum, the exploitation of the prostitution of others or other forms of sexual exploitation, forced labour or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, servitude or the removal of organs.
What are the two primary categories of Human Trafficking as stated by the Victims of Trafficking and Violence Protection Act?	Sex trafficking, labor trafficking
What are the three vital components of Human Trafficking?	Force, Fraud, Coercion
What term is defined by the use of a weapon; the use of such physical strength or violence as is sufficient to overcome, restrain, or injure a person; or inflicting physical harm sufficient to coerce or compel submission by the victim?	FORCE
What is the definition of the term FRAUD as stated in BPOC?	A deceitful practice or willful device, resorted to with intent to deprive another of his right, or in some manner to do him an injury.
What are some behaviors that indicate COERCION?	threats of serious harm to or physical restraint against any person; Any scheme, plan, or pattern intended to cause a person to believe that failure to perform an act would result in serious harm to or physical restraint against any person; the abuse or threatened abuse of the legal process.
True or False: Human Trafficking can only be committed against adults.	False. Trafficking involves children and adults of all ages.
What is everpresent in recruitment methods of traffickers?	Lying (about opportunities, legitimate employment, money, etc.)
True or False: Human Trafficking is only a problem in third-world countries.	False. While many victims can come from economically disadvantaged countries, richer countries are the primary "destination countries" making human trafficking a global problem.
Name some more vulnerable communities to Human Trafficking.	Runaways, LGBTQ+, African American, Latino, children in foster care
What organization is abbreviated by the acronym OJJDP?	The Office of Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention
What activities are defined by the phrase Commercial Sexual Exploitation of Children (CSEC)?	the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child for the financial benefit of any person or in exchange for anything of value (including monetary and non-monetary benefits) given or received by any person.
What are some examples of Commercial Sexual Exploitation of Children (CSEC)?	child sex trafficking, the prostitution of children, child sex tourism involving commercial sexual activity, commercial production of child pornography, and online transmission of live video of a child engaged in sexual activity
How is CHILD SEX TRAFFICKING defined in BPOC?	Child sex trafficking occurs when a person KNOWINGLY: Traffics a child under the age of 18 and causes by any means the child to engage in or become the victim of commercial sex acts or child sex abuse; Receives a benefit from participating in a venture that involves child sex trafficking; or Engages in sexual conduct with a trafficked child.
ABUSE can involve what acts or ommissions?	compelling or encouraging the child to engage in sexual conduct as defined by Section 43.01, Penal Code, including compelling or encouraging the child in a manner that constitutes an offense of trafficking of persons under Section 20A.02(a)(7); Penal Code, prostitution under Section 43.02 Penal Code, or compelling prostitution under Section 43.05(a)(2), Penal Code; knowingly causing, permitting, encouraging, engaging in, or allowing a child to be trafficked in a manner punishable as an offense under Section 20A.02(a)(5), (6), (7), or (8) Penal Code; or the failure to make a reasonable effort to prevent a child from being trafficked in a manner punishable as an offense under any of those sections.
True or False: It is a defense to prosecution for a Human Trafficking offense against a child if the actor did not know the age of the child.	False.
How is CONSENT defined in this chapter of BPOC?	The instance when one person voluntarily agrees to the proposal or desires of another.
Describe the main difference between TRAFFICKING and SMUGGLING.	TRAFFICKING is a crime against a HUMAN, and the exploitation of the person. SMUGGLING involves moving a human or humans across BORDERS, and is a crime against a COUNTRY.
What age does a person need to be to be considered a CHILD in terms of trafficking?	Under 18 years of age OTHERWISE PHRASED AS 17 years of age or younger.
What three-word term is defined as "The recruitment, transportation, transfer, harbouring or receipt of persons, by means of the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, of abduction, of fraud, of deception, of the abuse of power or of a position of vulnerability or of the giving or receiving of payments or benefits to achieve the consent of a person having control over another person, for the purpose of exploitation"	Trafficking in persons
True or False: A consensual sex worker is not invloved in human trafficking; A trafficking victim is a sexual assault victim.	True.

# LO 21.9
Name some behavioral indicators that can indicate a victim of trafficking.	Disconnect from family, friends, and community; sudden dramatic change in behavior; stops attending school; bruises in various stages of healing; deferring to another person when questioned; absence of freedom of movement; frightened, resistant, or belligerent to law enforcement; use of trafficking related terms like "trick," "the life," or "the game"; child preoccupied with making money; no stable living situation; tattoos/branding
When engaging with potential trafficking victims, it is important to ask questions in what fashion?	a non-threatening and non- authoritative manner
What are important questions that can be asked related to safety and/or coercion?	What is your understanding of what would happen if you tried to leave? Have you been physically harmed, deprived of food, water, sleep, medical care, or other life necessities? Can you come and go as you please? Are you free to have a relationship with someone? Can you freely contact (phone, write, email) friends and family?

What percentage of communication is communicated at a non-verbal level?	70%
What are some examples of non-verbal communication from a potential trafficking victim?	Dryness of mouth, Restlessness, Excessive sweating, Posture, Hand movements/fidgeting
Analysis of non-verbal cues is known as _______ interviewing.	Kinesic

# LO 21.17
When a sex trafficking offense is committed against a _____, it is treated like a felony statutory sexual assault.	Child 
Define sex trafficking.	the recruitment, harboring, transportation, provision, obtaining, patronizing, or soliciting of a person for the purpose of a commercial sex act in which a commercial sex act is induced by force, fraud, or coercion, or in which the person forced to perform such an act is under the age of eighteen years old
Define labor trafficking.	the recruitment, harboring, transportation, provision, or obtaining of a person for labor services, through the use of force, fraud, or coercion for the purpose of subjection to involuntary servitude, peonage, debt bondage or slavery
Define document servitude.	holding an actual or false identity document of a victim while committing a trafficking offense
What is the RICO statute?	The Federal Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations Act, allowing victims to sue their captors in federal courts.
How many states now have anti-trafficking laws?	All 50.
What does the TVPA, in effect, do?	Creates laws that criminalize trafficking regarding slavery, involuntary servitude, peonage or forced labor; Permits prosecution where non-violent coercion is used to force victims to work in belief they would be subject to serious harm; Permits prosecution where victim's service is compelled by confiscation of documents such as passports or birth certificates; Requires courts to order restitution and forfeiture of assets upon conviction; Enables victims to seek witness protection and other types of assistance; Gives prosecutors and agents new tools to get legal immigration status for victims of trafficking during investigation and prosecution
What increases prison terms for all slavery violations and adds life imprisonment where violation involves death, kidnapping, or sexual abuse of victim?	The TVPA 

# LO 21.18
A T-visa allows victims of severe forms of trafficking to do what?	To remain in the United States for four years and at the end of four years, T-visa holders may apply for permanent residence.
What form must be submitted for a T-Visa?	I-914 Application for T Nonimmigrant Status
Proof of victimization generally has what two forms?	a personal narrative; a letter by a federal law enforcement officer attesting to the victims claims of victimization
A personal narrative must contain what information?	What were the circumstances of their entry into the U.S.; The purpose for which they were brought into the U.S.; How they were recruited or otherwise became involved in the trafficking situation; When the events took place; Who was responsible; How long they were detained by the traffickers; How and when they escaped, were rescued, or otherwise became separated from the traffickers; What they have been doing since they were separated from the traffickers; Why they were unable to leave the U.S. after being separated from the traffickers; What harm or mistreatment they fear if they were removed from the U.S.; Why they fear they would be harmed or mistreated
Victims are encouraged to also submit which supplemental form for a T-Visa?	Form I-914, Supplement B, Declaration of Law Enforcement Officer for Victim of Trafficking in Persons.
True or False: Law enforcement may begin the process of obtaining a T-Visa for a victim of trafficking.	False. Victims must self-petition and must begin the process for T-visas rather than waiting for law enforcement to begin this process for them
True or False: T-Visa applicants must agree to cooperate in order to stay in the U.S.	True.
The maximum number of T-visa applicants whose status may be adjusted to that of permanent resident per year is ____ and does not include family members.	5,000
