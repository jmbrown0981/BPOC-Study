## True or False
### LO 17.3
True or False: Sex workers are considered a high-risk population for sexual assault.  Answer: True  Explanation: Being a sex worker does not automatically imply consent. In addition to providing sex workers with the same quality of investigation as any other persons, if human trafficking is suspected, the officer should follow the agency or department reporting protocols.

### LO 17.4
True or False: A trauma-informed approach reminds us that not anyone can be a trauma survivor.  Answer: False Explanation: Traumatic experiences can happen to anyone, making it possible for anyone to be a trauma survivor.

### LO 17.5
True or False: Meeting a victim's basic needs and concerns includes allowing family members, friends, or suspects to act as interpreters. Answer: False Explanation: While victims may need interpreters, family members, friends, and suspects should not be allowed to act in this capacity.

### LO 17.12
True or False: Family violence calls are one of the least dangerous for responding officers.    Answer: False   Explanation: The opposite is true. They are one of the most dangerous types of calls for responding officers.

True or False: The caller may become hostile towards the officers once a decision for an arrest has been made.  Answer: True    Explanation: Family dynamics make both the victim and the suspect more unpredictable than parties involved in other crimes.

True or False: Most family violence calls are very simple, because stories from all parties often align.    Answer: False   Explanation: Family violence calls often involve two subjects providing contradicting narratives regarding an unverifiable event.
