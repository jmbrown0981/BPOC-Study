§50.02 – UNLAWFUL USE OF FIREWORKS

Setting off fireworks to obstruct a law enforcement officer or to help escape arrest.

A person commits this offense if they explode or ignite fireworks with the intent to:

  • Interfere with the lawful performance of an official duty by a law enforcement officer, OR
  • Flee from a person the suspect knows is a law enforcement officer who is attempting to lawfully arrest or detain them

"Law enforcement officer" includes Texas peace officers and federal law enforcement officers.

Enhancement Ladder

  • State Jail Felony (base) — using any consumer fireworks to interfere with or flee from an officer
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — if the fireworks used were not consumer fireworks (i.e., commercial-grade or prohibited fireworks)
  • Felony of the 1st Degree — if the offense causes serious bodily injury to a law enforcement officer the suspect knows is lawfully discharging an official duty, or if done in retaliation for the officer's exercise of official power or performance of duty

Note: If the same conduct violates another law, the suspect can be prosecuted under this section, the other law, or both.
