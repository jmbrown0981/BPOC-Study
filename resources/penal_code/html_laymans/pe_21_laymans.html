§21.02 CONTINUOUS SEXUAL ABUSE OF YOUNG CHILD OR DISABLED INDIVIDUAL ▾

§21.02 – CONTINUOUS SEXUAL ABUSE OF YOUNG CHILD OR DISABLED INDIVIDUAL

Committing multiple acts of sexual abuse over 30+ days against a child under 14 or a disabled individual — treated as a single, extremely serious offense regardless of whether all the specific acts are proven.

Elements

  • During a period of 30 or more days, the suspect commits two or more acts of sexual abuse (can be against one or more victims).
  • At the time of each act, the suspect was 17 years of age or older.
  • The victim was: (a) a child younger than 14 — it does not matter if the suspect did not know the victim's age; OR (b) a disabled individual.

What Counts as an "Act of Sexual Abuse"

Any act that violates one or more of these statutes:

  • Aggravated kidnapping (§20.04(a)(4)) with intent to sexually abuse
  • Indecency with a Child (§21.11(a)(1)) — contact-type (not mere exposure)
  • Sexual Assault (§22.011)
  • Aggravated Sexual Assault (§22.021)
  • Burglary with intent to commit any of the above
  • Sexual Performance by a Child (§43.25)
  • Trafficking of Persons involving a child's sexual exploitation
  • Compelling Prostitution (§43.05)

Jury Note

Jurors do NOT have to agree on which specific acts occurred or the exact dates — they only need to unanimously agree that at least two acts of sexual abuse occurred over 30+ days.

Punishment

  • Felony of the 1st Degree, punishable by 25 years to 99 years or life.

Affirmative Defense (Young Victims Only)

If the victims were all under 14, the suspect may raise an affirmative defense if: (1) the suspect was not more than 5 years older than the youngest victim, (2) the suspect used no duress, force, or threat, and (3) at the time of the offense the suspect was not a registered-for-life sex offender and had no prior reportable conviction under this section.

§21.03 CONTINUOUS SEXUAL ABUSE ▾

§21.03 – CONTINUOUS SEXUAL ABUSE

Committing multiple acts of sexual abuse over 30+ days against two or more victims — similar to §21.02 but focused on multiple victims rather than young children.

Elements

  • During a period of 30 or more days, the suspect commits two or more acts of sexual abuse against two or more victims.
  • At the time of each act, the suspect was 17 years of age or older.

What Counts as an "Act of Sexual Abuse"

Same list as §21.02 — aggravated kidnapping with sexual intent, indecency with a child (contact-type), sexual assault, aggravated sexual assault, qualifying burglary, sexual performance by a child, sex trafficking, or compelling prostitution.

Jury Note

Jurors must unanimously agree that the defendant committed two or more acts of sexual abuse against two or more victims over 30+ days — but do not need to agree on specific acts or exact dates.

Punishment

  • Felony of the 1st Degree

Affirmative Defense (Victims Under 17)

If all victims were under 17, the suspect may raise an affirmative defense if: (1) not more than 5 years older than the youngest victim, (2) used no duress, force, or threats, and (3) was not a registered-for-life sex offender and had no prior reportable conviction under this section.

Note: This section was added in 2025.

§21.06 HOMOSEXUAL CONDUCT ▾

§21.06 – HOMOSEXUAL CONDUCT

This section was declared unconstitutional by the U.S. Supreme Court in Lawrence v. Texas (2003) and is NOT enforceable.

The statute (Class C misdemeanor for deviate sexual intercourse between same-sex individuals) remains in the code but cannot be charged, prosecuted, or enforced.

§21.07 PUBLIC LEWDNESS ▾

§21.07 – PUBLIC LEWDNESS

Engaging in sexual activity — intercourse, deviate sexual intercourse, or sexual contact — in public or where the suspect should have known someone would be offended.

Elements

The suspect knowingly engages in one of these acts:

  • Sexual intercourse
  • Deviate sexual intercourse (mouth-genitals/anus contact, or penetration with an object)
  • Sexual contact (touching genitals, anus, or breast with sexual intent)

AND either: (a) they are in a public place, OR (b) they are not in public but are reckless about whether another person present will be offended or alarmed.

Punishment

  • Base offense: Class A Misdemeanor
  • Enhancement — Felony of the 3rd Degree: The suspect is civilly committed as a sexually violent predator under Chapter 841, Health & Safety Code.
§21.08 INDECENT EXPOSURE ▾

§21.08 – INDECENT EXPOSURE

Exposing genitals or anus with sexual intent while being reckless about offending someone who can see it.

Elements

The suspect exposes their anus or genitals with the intent to arouse or gratify sexual desire of any person, AND is reckless about whether another person present will be offended or alarmed.

Punishment

  • Base offense: Class B Misdemeanor
  • Enhancement — Class A Misdemeanor: Suspect has been previously convicted one time of this offense.
  • Enhancement — State Jail Felony: Suspect has been previously convicted two or more times of this offense.
  • Enhancement — Felony of the 3rd Degree: The suspect is civilly committed as a sexually violent predator.
§21.09 BESTIALITY ▾

§21.09 – BESTIALITY

Knowingly engaging in sexual contact with an animal, facilitating others doing so, or doing it where a child can see.

Elements — Any of the Following

  • Sexual contact between the person's mouth, anus, or genitals and the anus or genitals of an animal (or vice versa)
  • Fondling or touching an animal's genitals or anus in a non-husbandry/non-veterinary manner
  • Causing an animal to contact the person's seminal fluid
  • Inserting any body part or object into an animal's anus or genitals in a non-husbandry/non-veterinary manner
  • Possessing, selling, transferring, or purchasing an animal with intent to use it for bestiality
  • Organizing, promoting, conducting, or observing bestiality
  • Causing or helping another person engage in bestiality
  • Permitting bestiality on property under the person's control
  • Committing bestiality in the presence of a child under 18
  • Advertising or offering to provide an animal for use in bestiality

Punishment

  • Base offense: State Jail Felony
  • Enhancement — Felony of the 2nd Degree: Committed in the presence of a child under 18 (subsection (a)(9)), OR resulted in serious bodily injury or death to the animal.

Exception

The offense does not apply to generally accepted, lawful animal husbandry or veterinary practices.

§21.11 INDECENCY WITH A CHILD ▾

§21.11 – INDECENCY WITH A CHILD

Engaging in sexual contact with, or exposing genitals to, a child under 17 — regardless of whether the suspect knew the child's age.

Track A — Sexual Contact

The suspect engages in sexual contact with a child under 17, or causes the child to engage in sexual contact. "Sexual contact" means touching the anus, breast, or genitals of the child — or touching any part of the child's body with the suspect's anus, breast, or genitals — with intent to arouse or gratify sexual desire. This includes touching through clothing.

  • Punishment: Felony of the 2nd Degree

Track B — Exposure

The suspect, with intent to arouse or gratify sexual desire of any person, either: (a) exposes their own anus or genitals while knowing the child is present, OR (b) causes the child to expose their own anus or genitals.

  • Punishment: Felony of the 3rd Degree

Key Point on Age

The suspect does not need to have known the victim was under 17 at the time of the offense. Age is irrelevant to the suspect's knowledge.

Affirmative Defenses

  • The actor was not more than 3 years older than the victim, was of the opposite sex, used no duress/force/threats, and was not a registered-for-life sex offender with a prior conviction under this section. ("Romeo and Juliet" defense)
  • The actor was the spouse of the child at the time of the offense.
§21.12 IMPROPER RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATOR AND STUDENT ▾

§21.12 – IMPROPER RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATOR AND STUDENT

A school employee having sex with — or engaging in online sexual conduct with — a student.

Who Can Commit It

An employee of a public or private primary or secondary school commits this offense.

Three Ways to Commit

  • Track 1: Engages in sexual contact, sexual intercourse, or deviate sexual intercourse with a person enrolled in the school where the employee works.
  • Track 2: Is in a certified teaching/education position and engages in those acts with a person the employee knows is enrolled in any other school, or is a student participant in a school-sponsored educational activity.
  • Track 3: Engages in online sexual solicitation (§33.021) with a person described in Tracks 1 or 2 — regardless of the person's age.

Punishment

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree

Affirmative Defenses

  • The actor was the spouse of the enrolled person at the time of the offense.
  • The actor was not more than 3 years older than the enrolled person AND the relationship began before the actor's employment at a school.

Privacy Note

The student's name cannot be publicly released. The accused employee's name cannot be released publicly until indicted, except for reporting to agencies, law enforcement, or school community per policy.

§21.15 INVASIVE VISUAL RECORDING ▾

§21.15 – INVASIVE VISUAL RECORDING

Secretly recording, photographing, or broadcasting someone's intimate areas or someone undressing in a private space — or distributing such recordings.

Three Ways to Commit

  • Track 1 — Recording Intimate Areas: Without the other person's consent and with intent to invade their privacy, photographing or electronically recording/broadcasting an intimate area (genitals, pubic area, anus, buttocks, or female breast below the areola) of someone who reasonably expected that area not to be publicly viewed.
  • Track 2 — Recording in Private Space: Without consent and with intent to invade privacy, recording or broadcasting a person in a place where they have a reasonable expectation of privacy — such as a bathroom, bedroom, or changing room. (A posted sign is NOT sufficient consent.)
  • Track 3 — Promoting Such Recordings: Knowing the character and content of the recording, distributing or promoting a recording described in Track 1 or 2.

Punishment

  • State Jail Felony
§21.16 UNLAWFUL DISCLOSURE OR PROMOTION OF INTIMATE VISUAL MATERIAL ▾

§21.16 – UNLAWFUL DISCLOSURE OR PROMOTION OF INTIMATE VISUAL MATERIAL

Commonly called "revenge porn" — sharing intimate images of someone without their consent to harm them, threatening to share such images, or running a platform that publishes such material.

Three Tracks

  • Track 1 — Non-Consensual Disclosure (Revenge Porn): Without effective consent and with intent to harm the depicted person, disclosing visual material showing the person's intimate parts exposed or engaged in sexual conduct; knowing the material was obtained under circumstances where the person expected it to remain private; the disclosure causes harm; and the disclosure reveals the person's identity.
  • Track 2 — Threatening to Disclose for Benefit: Intentionally threatening to disclose intimate visual material without consent, to obtain something in return (e.g., money, more images, sexual acts).
  • Track 3 — Promoting on a Website: Knowing the character and content, promoting such intimate visual material on an internet website or other publication forum that the person owns or operates.

Key Points

  • It is NOT a defense that the depicted person originally created the material or voluntarily sent it to the suspect.

Punishment

  • State Jail Felony

Affirmative Defenses (Tracks 1 and 3)

  • The disclosure was made in the course of lawful law enforcement practices, reporting unlawful activity, or a legal proceeding.
  • The material shows only a person voluntarily exposing themselves in a public or commercial setting.
  • The actor is an interactive computer service (e.g., a platform like a social media company) and the material was provided by a third party.
§21.165 UNLAWFUL PRODUCTION OR DISTRIBUTION OF CERTAIN SEXUALLY EXPLICIT MEDIA ▾

§21.165 – UNLAWFUL PRODUCTION OR DISTRIBUTION OF CERTAIN SEXUALLY EXPLICIT MEDIA

Creating or distributing AI-generated or digitally manipulated "deepfake" pornographic content depicting a real person without their consent.

Elements — Track 1 (Production/Distribution)

Without the effective consent of the depicted person, the suspect knowingly produces or electronically distributes deepfake media that appears to depict the person: (a) with visible AI-generated or substituted intimate parts, OR (b) engaging in sexual conduct that the person did not actually perform.

Elements — Track 2 (Threatening)

The suspect intentionally threatens to produce or distribute deepfake media to coerce, extort, harass, or intimidate another person.

Consent Requirement

Consent is only valid if the depicted person signed a written plain-language agreement describing the media and, if applicable, the work it will be incorporated into.

Punishment — Track 1

  • Base: Class A Misdemeanor
  • Enhancement — Felony of the 3rd Degree: Prior conviction under this section, OR the depicted person is under 18.

Punishment — Track 2

  • Base: Class B Misdemeanor
  • Enhancement — Class A Misdemeanor: Prior conviction, OR the threatened deepfake depicted someone under 18.

Notable Rules

  • It is NOT a defense that the deepfake contains a disclaimer saying it is unauthorized or fake.
  • Convicted defendants must pay restitution for psychological, financial, or reputational harm to the victim.
§21.17 VOYEURISM ▾

§21.17 – VOYEURISM

Secretly watching another person in a private setting — like a home, room, or dwelling — for sexual gratification, without their consent.

Elements

With the intent to arouse or gratify the sexual desire of the suspect, the suspect observes (including remotely via electronic means) another person without their consent while the person is in a dwelling or structure where they have a reasonable expectation of privacy.

Punishment

  • Base offense: Class A Misdemeanor
  • Enhancement — State Jail Felony: Prior conviction under this section; OR the victim was a child under 18 at the time; OR the offense occurred on the premises of a postsecondary educational institution.
  • Enhancement — Felony of the 3rd Degree: The victim was under 18 AND the suspect has a prior conviction for an offense punishable as a state jail felony because the victim was under 18.
§21.18 SEXUAL COERCION ▾

§21.18 – SEXUAL COERCION

Extorting or threatening someone with a sex crime or violent crime in order to obtain intimate images, sexual acts, or money.

Track 1 — Threatening a Sex Offense to Get Something

The suspect intentionally threatens (through coercion or extortion) to commit a sex-related offense (including trafficking, prostitution-related offenses, or sexual assaults) to obtain one of the following in exchange for not following through:

  • Intimate visual material (nude or sexual images)
  • An act involving sexual conduct
  • A monetary or other benefit of value

Track 2 — Threatening a Violent Crime to Get Sexual Content

The suspect intentionally threatens to commit homicide, kidnapping, or trafficking (non-sexual labor type) to obtain intimate visual material or sexual conduct from the victim.

Communication Method

Applies to threats made in any form — in person, email, social media, chat rooms, or other electronic means.

Punishment

  • Base offense: State Jail Felony
  • Enhancement — Felony of the 3rd Degree: Prior conviction under this section.
§21.19 UNLAWFUL ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION OF SEXUALLY EXPLICIT VISUAL MATERIAL ▾

§21.19 – UNLAWFUL ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION OF SEXUALLY EXPLICIT VISUAL MATERIAL

Sending unsolicited explicit images or videos electronically — commonly called "cyberflashing" or sending unsolicited explicit content.

Elements

The suspect knowingly transmits by electronic means visual material that depicts: (a) any person engaging in sexual conduct or with intimate parts exposed, OR (b) the covered but visibly aroused male genitals — AND the material was not sent at the request of or with the express consent of the recipient.

Punishment

  • Class C Misdemeanor
