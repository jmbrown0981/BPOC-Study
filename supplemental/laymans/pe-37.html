§37.02 PERJURY ▾

§37.02 – PERJURY

Lying under oath — or in an unsworn declaration — when you know the statement is false and intend to deceive.

The suspect, with intent to deceive and knowing the statement's meaning, either: (1) makes a false statement under oath (or swears a previously made false statement is true) when law requires it to be under oath; or (2) makes a false unsworn declaration under Chapter 132, Civil Practice and Remedies Code.

  • Class A Misdemeanor – base and only grade
§37.03 AGGRAVATED PERJURY ▾

§37.03 – AGGRAVATED PERJURY

Lying under oath during an official proceeding when the lie is material — meaning it could affect the outcome of the proceeding.

The suspect commits perjury (§37.02) AND the false statement: (1) is made during or in connection with an official proceeding; AND (2) is material (could have affected the course or outcome of the proceeding). Whether a statement is material is a question of law for the judge, not the jury.

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – base and only grade

Defense (§37.05 – Retraction): It is a defense if the suspect retracted the false statement before the testimony at the proceeding was complete, AND before it became apparent that the lie was about to be exposed.

Notable (§37.06): If a suspect made two sworn statements that cannot both be true, the prosecution doesn't have to prove which one was false — both can be charged and prosecuted.

§37.08 FALSE REPORT TO PEACE OFFICER, FEDERAL SPECIAL INVESTIGATOR, LAW ENFORCEMENT EMPLOYEE, CORRECTIONS OFFICER, OR JAILER ▾

§37.08 – FALSE REPORT TO PEACE OFFICER, FEDERAL SPECIAL INVESTIGATOR, LAW ENFORCEMENT EMPLOYEE, CORRECTIONS OFFICER, OR JAILER

Lying to a police officer, investigator, or corrections officer during a criminal investigation — with intent to deceive.

The suspect, with intent to deceive, knowingly makes a false statement that is material to a criminal investigation, and makes it to: a peace officer or federal special investigator conducting the investigation; an authorized law enforcement agency employee conducting the investigation (who the suspect knows is doing so); or a corrections officer or jailer.

  • Class B Misdemeanor – base and only grade
§37.081 FALSE REPORT REGARDING MISSING CHILD OR MISSING PERSON ▾

§37.081 – FALSE REPORT REGARDING MISSING CHILD OR MISSING PERSON

Filing a false missing persons report or lying to police about a missing child or person.

The suspect, with intent to deceive, knowingly either: files a false missing child or missing person report with law enforcement; or makes a false statement to a law enforcement officer or employee about a missing child or person.

  • Class C Misdemeanor – base and only grade
§37.082 MISREPRESENTING CHILD AS FAMILY MEMBER AT PORT OF ENTRY ▾

§37.082 – MISREPRESENTING CHILD AS FAMILY MEMBER AT PORT OF ENTRY

Falsely telling officers at a border crossing that a child is your family member — with intent to traffic that child.

The suspect, with intent to commit trafficking of persons (§20A.02), knowingly misrepresents a child (under 18) as their family member to a peace officer or federal special investigator at a U.S. port of entry.

  • Class B Misdemeanor – base and only grade
§37.09 TAMPERING WITH OR FABRICATING PHYSICAL EVIDENCE ▾

§37.09 – TAMPERING WITH OR FABRICATING PHYSICAL EVIDENCE

Destroying, hiding, or faking physical evidence during an ongoing investigation or proceeding — or concealing a body from law enforcement.

Track A – While Investigation or Proceeding is Underway (sub.(a))

The suspect knows an investigation or official proceeding is pending or in progress, and either: destroys, alters, or conceals any record, document, or thing to impair its availability or integrity as evidence; or makes, presents, or uses a false record/document/thing with intent to affect the investigation's outcome.

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – base grade (altering/concealing)
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree – if the item concealed or altered is a human corpse
  • Class A Misdemeanor – for making/presenting/using a false record (sub.(a)(2))

Track B – After an Offense Has Been Committed (sub.(d)(1))

The suspect knows an offense has been committed and conceals/destroys evidence to impair its availability in a subsequent investigation.

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – base grade
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree – if what is concealed is a human corpse

Track C – Failing to Report a Corpse (sub.(d)(2))

The suspect sees a human body under circumstances where a reasonable person would believe an offense was committed, knows police don't know about the body, and fails to report its existence and location to law enforcement.

  • Class A Misdemeanor

Exceptions: Privileged communications and attorney work product are excluded. Legally destroying visual material under §43.261(f)(3) is also a defense.

§37.10 TAMPERING WITH GOVERNMENTAL RECORD ▾

§37.10 – TAMPERING WITH GOVERNMENTAL RECORD

Falsifying, destroying, selling, or misusing official government records and documents — courts, licenses, permits, election records, etc.

The suspect knowingly: makes a false entry in or alteration of a government record; presents or uses a fake government record; intentionally destroys, conceals, removes, or impairs a government record; possesses or sells government record forms for unlawful use; or possesses government records obtained unlawfully.

Base Grades

  • Class A Misdemeanor – base grade (no intent to defraud)
  • State Jail Felony – if the suspect intended to defraud or harm another

Special Record Type Enhancements

  • Class C Misdemeanor – if the record is a school enrollment residency document
  • Class B Misdemeanor – if the record is a tax appraisal filed with a conflicted appraiser, or a ballot application with false candidate information, or a temporary vehicle tag
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – if the record is a public school assessment or PEIMS report; a government-issued license/certificate/permit/title/patent; an expert examination report in a criminal case; an instrument calibration record in a criminal case; or a search warrant
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree – if the record in the previous category was tampered with and the suspect intended to defraud (upgrades from F3)

Insurance Document Track (§37.01(2)(D) records)

  • Class B Misdemeanor – presenting or using a fake insurance document
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – making a fake insurance document, or other tampering acts
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree – if the intent was to defraud or harm another

Defense: For false entries or false information, it is a defense that the false entry could have no effect on the government's purpose for requiring the record.

Presumption: Acting with respect to two or more of the same type of government-issued licenses, certificates, or permits creates a presumption of intent to defraud.

§37.101 FRAUDULENT FILING OF FINANCING STATEMENT ▾

§37.101 – FRAUDULENT FILING OF FINANCING STATEMENT

Filing a forged, false, or baseless UCC financing statement (a legal lien document) with the government.

The suspect knowingly presents for filing a financing statement that they know is: forged; contains a material false statement; or is groundless (has no legal basis).

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – if the financing statement is forged
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree – if forged AND the suspect has 2 or more prior convictions under this section
  • Class A Misdemeanor – if the statement contains a false statement or is groundless
  • State Jail Felony – if the false-statement or groundless filing was done with intent to defraud or harm another
§37.11 IMPERSONATING PUBLIC SERVANT ▾

§37.11 – IMPERSONATING PUBLIC SERVANT

Pretending to be a police officer, judge, or other government official to get someone to submit to fake authority or rely on fake official acts.

Two forms: (1) the suspect impersonates a public servant with intent to get someone to submit to fake official authority or rely on fake official acts; (2) the suspect knowingly exercises government functions (including judicial functions) without legal authority.

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – base and only grade
§37.12 FALSE IDENTIFICATION AS PEACE OFFICER; MISREPRESENTATION OF PROPERTY ▾

§37.12 – FALSE IDENTIFICATION AS PEACE OFFICER; MISREPRESENTATION OF PROPERTY

Making, giving, or possessing fake police badges or insignia for someone who isn't actually a commissioned peace officer — or disguising a vehicle as a law enforcement vehicle.

Two forms: (1) the suspect makes, provides, or possesses an item (badge, ID, shoulder patch, vehicle marking) bearing a law enforcement agency insignia that identifies someone as a peace officer, knowing that person is NOT actually commissioned as one; (2) the suspect intentionally misrepresents an object as property of a law enforcement agency.

  • Class B Misdemeanor – base and only grade for both forms

Defenses: The item clearly labels the person as an honorary/junior peace officer or member of a junior posse; the person was actually commissioned at the time the item was made; the item is used exclusively for decorative purposes or in an artistic or dramatic presentation.

§37.13 RECORD OF A FRAUDULENT COURT ▾

§37.13 – RECORD OF A FRAUDULENT COURT

Creating or using fake "court orders" from made-up courts that have no legal existence under state or federal law — a tool of sovereign citizen/paper terrorism schemes.

The suspect makes, presents, or uses any document knowing it is NOT a record of a real court created under state or federal law, with the intent that it be given the same legal effect as a real court record.

  • Class A Misdemeanor – base grade
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – if the suspect has 2 or more prior convictions under this section
§37.14 FALSE STATEMENT REGARDING CHILD CUSTODY DETERMINATION MADE IN FOREIGN COUNTRY ▾

§37.14 – FALSE STATEMENT REGARDING CHILD CUSTODY DETERMINATION MADE IN FOREIGN COUNTRY

Lying to a Texas court about a foreign country's child custody ruling during a hearing on that custody matter.

The suspect knowingly makes or causes a false statement to be made about a child custody determination issued by a foreign country, during a Texas court hearing under Chapter 152 (Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction) or Chapter 153 (Subchapter I), Family Code.

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree – base and only grade
