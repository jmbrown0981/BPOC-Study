§43.02 PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.02 – PROSTITUTION

Offering or agreeing to accept payment in exchange for sexual conduct (the person selling the service).

  • Class B Misdemeanor — base offense: knowingly offering or agreeing to receive a fee from another to engage in sexual conduct.
  • Class A Misdemeanor — if the person has 1 or 2 prior prostitution convictions.
  • State Jail Felony — if the person has 3 or more prior prostitution convictions.

Defense: It is a defense that the actor engaged in prostitution because they were a victim of trafficking (§20A.02) or compelling prostitution (§43.05).

§43.021 SOLICITATION OF PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.021 – SOLICITATION OF PROSTITUTION

Offering or agreeing to pay for sex — the buyer's side of prostitution.

  • State Jail Felony — base offense: knowingly offering or agreeing to pay a fee to another person for the purpose of engaging in sexual conduct.
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — if the suspect has a prior solicitation conviction.
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — if the person they agreed to pay is younger than 18, represented as younger than 18, or believed by the suspect to be younger than 18 (regardless of actual age).

School zone enhancement: Punishment increases one category if the offense occurred within 1,000 feet of a school or official school function/UIL event.

§43.03 PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.03 – PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION

Running a prostitution operation — receiving money from it or soliciting clients for others (pimping).

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — base offense: receiving money from prostitution proceeds under an agreement, or soliciting another person to engage in sexual conduct for compensation (other than personally providing the service).
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — if the suspect has a prior promotion conviction.
  • Felony of the 1st Degree — if the person being prostituted is younger than 18, regardless of whether the suspect knew the age.
§43.031 ONLINE PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.031 – ONLINE PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION

Owning, managing, or operating a website or online service with the intent to promote prostitution or help others engage in or solicit prostitution.

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — base offense.
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — if the suspect has a prior conviction under this section or §43.041; or if the conduct involves a person younger than 18, regardless of whether the suspect knew the age.
§43.032 CONTINUOUS PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.032 – CONTINUOUS PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION

Running a prostitution promotion operation repeatedly over a sustained period — 30 or more days.

  • Felony of the 1st Degree — engaging in conduct constituting promotion of prostitution (§43.03) two or more times during a period of 30 or more days.

Note: If the victim of this offense is the same victim as in a §43.03 charge in the same case, the suspect cannot be convicted of both for the same conduct (unless the §43.03 charge is alternative, occurred outside the 30-day period, or is found to be a lesser included offense).

§43.04 AGGRAVATED PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.04 – AGGRAVATED PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION

Running a prostitution enterprise that uses two or more prostitutes — organized prostitution at scale.

  • Felony of the 1st Degree — knowingly owning, investing in, financing, controlling, supervising, or managing a prostitution enterprise using two or more prostitutes.
§43.041 AGGRAVATED ONLINE PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.041 – AGGRAVATED ONLINE PROMOTION OF PROSTITUTION

Running a website or online service to promote prostitution involving five or more people.

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — base offense: owning, managing, or operating an online service to promote prostitution of five or more persons or facilitate five or more persons to engage in prostitution or solicitation.
  • Felony of the 1st Degree — if the suspect has a prior conviction under this section; or if two or more of the people involved are younger than 18, regardless of whether the suspect knew their age.
§43.05 COMPELLING PROSTITUTION ▾

§43.05 – COMPELLING PROSTITUTION

Forcing, tricking, or coercing someone into prostitution — including any child or disabled person.

  • Felony of the 1st Degree — all forms of compelling prostitution:
    • Causing another to commit prostitution by force, threat, coercion, or fraud;
    • Causing a child younger than 18 to commit prostitution, by any means (age of the child is not required knowledge);
    • Causing a disabled individual to commit prostitution, by any means.

Note on "coercion": Includes destroying/withholding government ID, intoxicating the victim to the point of impairing judgment, or withholding alcohol/drugs from an addicted person to control them.

§43.22 OBSCENE DISPLAY OR DISTRIBUTION ▾

§43.22 – OBSCENE DISPLAY OR DISTRIBUTION

Showing or handing out obscene photos, drawings, or similar visual material while being reckless about whether someone nearby will be offended.

  • Class C Misdemeanor — intentionally or knowingly displaying or distributing an obscene photograph, drawing, or similar visual material while being reckless about whether a person present will be offended or alarmed.
§43.23 OBSCENITY ▾

§43.23 – OBSCENITY

Dealing in obscene material or devices — whether selling it wholesale or retail, promoting it, or producing obscene performances.

Track A – Wholesale promotion / possession to wholesale promote (§a)

  • State Jail Felony — knowingly wholesale promoting or possessing with intent to wholesale promote obscene material or obscene devices.

Track B – Retail promotion or production (§c)

  • Class A Misdemeanor — knowingly promoting or possessing with intent to promote obscene material/devices; or producing, presenting, directing, or participating in an obscene performance.

Enhancement – Content depicts minors

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — for either Track A or B, if the obscene material visually depicts sexual activities involving a child under 18, or an image virtually indistinguishable from a child under 18, or an image created using an identifiable child's likeness.

Affirmative defense: Possession or promotion for a bona fide medical, psychiatric, judicial, legislative, or law enforcement purpose.

§43.231 PROMOTION OR POSSESSION OF CHILD-LIKE SEX DOLL ▾

§43.231 – PROMOTION OR POSSESSION OF CHILD-LIKE SEX DOLL

Buying, selling, or possessing an anatomically correct doll designed to look like a child for sexual purposes.

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — promoting (selling/distributing) a child-like sex doll.
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — possessing one with intent to promote.
  • State Jail Felony — simply possessing a child-like sex doll.

Note: Possessing two or more creates a legal presumption of intent to promote.

Affirmative defense: Possession or promotion for a bona fide law enforcement purpose.

§43.235 POSSESSION, PROMOTION, OR PRODUCTION OF CERTAIN VISUAL MATERIAL APPEARING TO DEPICT CHILD ▾

§43.235 – POSSESSION, PROMOTION, OR PRODUCTION OF CERTAIN VISUAL MATERIAL APPEARING TO DEPICT CHILD

Possessing or promoting obscene visual material — including AI-generated images — that appears to show a child in sexual activities, even if no real child was used.

  • State Jail Felony — base offense: knowingly possessing, accessing with intent to view, or promoting obscene visual material (including cartoons, animations, or AI-generated images) that depicts an apparent child under 18 engaging in sexual activities; or using an actual child's image to train an AI to produce child pornography.
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — with one prior conviction under this section or §43.23, §43.26, §43.261, or §43.262.
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — with two or more prior convictions in the above categories.
§43.24 SALE, DISTRIBUTION, OR DISPLAY OF HARMFUL MATERIAL TO MINOR ▾

§43.24 – SALE, DISTRIBUTION, OR DISPLAY OF HARMFUL MATERIAL TO MINOR

Selling, showing, or distributing sexually explicit or otherwise harmful material to someone under 18.

  • Class A Misdemeanor — knowingly selling, distributing, exhibiting, or possessing for sale to a minor material whose dominant theme appeals to the prurient interest of minors in sex/nudity, is patently offensive, and has no redeeming social value for minors; or displaying such material recklessly where a minor may see it.
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — hiring, employing, or using a minor to sell or display the harmful material.

Defense: The actor was the spouse of the minor. Judicial or law enforcement officers acting in official duty also have an affirmative defense for selling/displaying.

§43.25 SEXUAL PERFORMANCE BY A CHILD ▾

§43.25 – SEXUAL PERFORMANCE BY A CHILD

Using, inducing, or directing a child under 18 to participate in sexual conduct or a sexual performance.

Track A – Employment/inducement (§b)

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — employing, authorizing, or inducing a child under 18 to engage in sexual conduct or a sexual performance; or a parent/guardian consenting to a child's participation in a sexual performance.
  • Felony of the 1st Degree — if the child is younger than 14 (regardless of whether the actor knew the age).

Track B – Producing/directing/promoting (§d)

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — producing, directing, or promoting a performance that includes sexual conduct by a child under 18.
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — if the child is younger than 14.

Note: Knowledge of the child's age is not required for conviction.

§43.251 EMPLOYMENT HARMFUL TO CHILDREN ▾

§43.251 – EMPLOYMENT HARMFUL TO CHILDREN

Hiring or forcing a child to work in a sexually oriented business or nude establishment.

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — employing, authorizing, or inducing a child (under 21) to work in a sexually oriented commercial activity (massage parlor, nude studio, love parlor, etc.) or any place of business that requires or permits the child to work nude or topless.
  • Felony of the 1st Degree — if the child is younger than 14 (regardless of whether the actor knew the age).
§43.26 POSSESSION OR PROMOTION OF CHILD PORNOGRAPHY ▾

§43.26 – POSSESSION OR PROMOTION OF CHILD PORNOGRAPHY

Possessing, accessing, or distributing visual depictions of children engaged in sexual conduct.

Possession of actual child imagery (§a-1)

  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — base: possessing or accessing with intent to view visual material depicting a real child under 18 engaging in sexual conduct.
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — with one prior sex offense conviction; or if the material contains 10–49 such depictions.
  • Felony of the 1st Degree — with two or more prior sex offense convictions; or if the material contains 50+ depictions; or if it depicts sexual conduct with a child under §22.011(a)(2).
  • Felony of the 1st Degree (25-year minimum) — if committed by a childcare/shelter employee or if displayed in a school library.
  • If the depicted child is under 10, punishments escalate one category.

Possession of computer-generated/AI imagery (§a-2)

  • State Jail Felony — base: possessing or accessing with intent to view AI-generated or computer-generated visual material that appears to be a child under 18 engaging in sexual conduct, and the person knows or believes it appears to depict a minor.
  • Escalates with prior convictions and quantity of images, up to a Felony of the 2nd Degree.

Promotion of child pornography (§e)

  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — base: promoting or possessing with intent to promote visual material depicting a real child under 18 engaging in sexual conduct.
  • Escalates to Felony of the 1st Degree with prior convictions, large quantities, or depictions of very young children.
§43.261 ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION OF CERTAIN VISUAL MATERIAL DEPICTING MINOR ▾

§43.261 – ELECTRONIC TRANSMISSION OF CERTAIN VISUAL MATERIAL DEPICTING MINOR (SEXTING – MINORS)

A minor electronically sending or possessing sexually explicit images of other minors — the "teen sexting" offense.

Only applies when the suspect is also a minor. Covers sending sexual images of minors via electronic means, or possessing such images that another minor produced.

Promoting (sending) – §b(1)

  • Class C Misdemeanor — base offense.
  • Class B Misdemeanor — if sent with harassment intent, or has one prior conviction.
  • Class A Misdemeanor — with prior harassment-motivated conviction or two or more prior convictions.

Possessing – §b(2)

  • Class C Misdemeanor — base offense.
  • Class B Misdemeanor — with one prior conviction.
  • Class A Misdemeanor — with two or more prior convictions.

Affirmative defense: If the image depicted only the actor and a close-in-age dating partner (within 2 years) or spouse, and was shared only between them.

§43.262 POSSESSION OR PROMOTION OF LEWD VISUAL MATERIAL DEPICTING CHILD ▾

§43.262 – POSSESSION OR PROMOTION OF LEWD VISUAL MATERIAL DEPICTING CHILD

Possessing or promoting sexually suggestive (but not necessarily pornographic) images of children — "child lewd material" that appeals to prurient interest and has no serious value.

  • State Jail Felony — base offense: knowingly possessing, accessing with intent to view, or promoting visual material depicting a child under 18 in a lewd exhibition of genitals or pubic area that appeals to prurient interest and has no serious literary, artistic, political, or scientific value (includes AI-generated images virtually indistinguishable from real children).
  • Felony of the 3rd Degree — with one prior conviction under this section or §43.26.
  • Felony of the 2nd Degree — with two or more prior convictions.
§43.28 CERTAIN SEXUALLY ORIENTED PERFORMANCES PROHIBITED ▾

§43.28 – CERTAIN SEXUALLY ORIENTED PERFORMANCES PROHIBITED

Performing a sexually oriented show in public where a child could see it, or performing in front of a child under 18.

"Sexually oriented performance" includes performances that feature nudity or explicit sexual conduct and appeal to prurient interest.

  • Class A Misdemeanor — engaging in a sexually oriented performance: (1) on public property in a manner that could reasonably be expected to be viewed by a child; or (2) in the presence of anyone younger than 18.
