HGN 1

Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus · Steps

Participant manual script

Tell the subject

  • Feet together, hands at sides
  • Head still
  • Look at stimulus
  • Follow movement with eyes
Before you start: have the subject remove glasses if worn, and hold the stimulus approximately 12″–15″ from the nose, just slightly above eye level.
HGN 2

Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus · Steps

Evaluation criteria

  1. Have subject remove glasses if worn.
  2. Gives proper verbal instructions.
  3. Stimulus held in proper position (approximately 12″–15″ from nose, just slightly above eye level).
  4. Check for equal pupil size and resting nystagmus.
  5. Check for equal tracking.
  6. Smooth movement from center of nose to maximum deviation in approximately 2 seconds and then back across subject’s face to maximum deviation in right eye, then back to center. Check left eye, then right eye. (Repeat)
  7. Eye held at maximum deviation for a minimum of 4 seconds (no white showing). Check left eye, then right eye. (Repeat)
  8. Eye moved slowly (approximately 4 seconds) from center to 45° angle. Check left eye, then right eye. (Repeat)
  9. Total the clues.
  10. Check for Vertical Gaze Nystagmus. (Repeat)
~2sec · center to max deviation (smooth pursuit) 4+sec · held at max deviation ~4sec · center to 45°
Order every time: left eye, then right eye — and repeat each pass.
HGN 3

Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus · Criteria 4–5

Pre-test medical evaluation

Before checking for clues, rule out medical explanations. These three checks come first.

1 · Equal pupil size

Unequal pupils can be a sign of head injury. Ask if the subject has experienced a head injury or any injury at this time.

2 · Resting nystagmus

Possible medical condition or a high dose of a dissociative anesthetic drug (PCP, ketamine).

3 · Equal tracking

Unequal tracking can indicate a medical condition (lazy eye, injury). Possibly not useful to continue testing if the eyes cannot track equally.

These checks correspond to evaluation criteria 4 (equal pupil size and resting nystagmus) and 5 (equal tracking).

WAT 1

Walk and Turn · Participant manual script

The instruction stance

  • Place your left foot on the line (real or imaginary).
  • Place your right foot on the line ahead of the left foot, with the heel of your right foot against the toe of the left foot.
  • Place your arms down at your sides.
  • Maintain this position until I have completed the instructions. Do not start to walk until told to do so.
  • Check understandingDo you understand the instructions so far? (Make sure subject indicates understanding.)
Clues 1 and 2 are scored during this instruction phase: the subject cannot keep balance, or starts too soon.
WAT 2

Walk and Turn · Participant manual script

The walking instructions

  • When I tell you to start, take nine heel-to-toe steps on the line, turn, and take nine heel-to-toe steps down the line.
  • When you turn, keep the front (lead) foot on the line, and turn by taking a series of small steps with the other foot, like this.
  • While you are walking, keep your arms at your sides, watch your feet at all times, and count your steps out loud.
  • Once you start walking, don’t stop until you have completed the test.
  • Check understandingDo you understand the instructions? (Make sure subject understands.)
  • BeginInstruct the person to begin the test.
WAT 3

Walk and Turn · Steps

Evaluation criteria

  1. Instructions given from a safe position.
  2. Tells subject to place feet on a line in heel-to-toe manner (left foot behind right foot) with arms at sides and gives demonstration.
  3. Tells subject not to begin test (“do not start walking”) until instructed to do so and asks if subject understands.
  4. Tells subject to take nine heel-to-toe steps on the line and demonstrates.
  5. Explains and demonstrates turning procedure.
  6. Tells subject to return on the line taking nine heel-to-toe steps.
  7. Tells subject to count steps out loud.
  8. Tells subject to look at feet while walking.
  9. Tells subject not to raise arms from sides.
  10. Tells subject not to stop walking once they begin.
  11. Asks subject if all instructions are understood.
Three demonstrations: the stance (2), the heel-to-toe steps (4), and the turn (5).

OLS 1

One Leg Stand · Steps

Participant manual script

  • Please stand with your feet together and your arms down at the sides, like this.
  • Do not start to perform the test until I tell you to do so.
  • Check understandingDo you understand the instructions so far?
  • When I tell you to start, raise either leg with the foot approximately six inches off the ground, keeping your foot parallel to the ground.
  • Keep both legs straight and your arms at your side.
  • While holding that position, count out loud in the following manner: “one thousand one, one thousand two, one thousand three,” and so on until told to stop.
  • Keep your arms at your sides at all times and keep watching the raised foot.
  • Check understandingDo you understand?
  • BeginGo ahead and perform the test. (Officer should always time the 30 seconds. Test should be discontinued after 30 seconds.)
OLS 2

One Leg Stand · Steps

Evaluation criteria

Instruction phase

  1. Instructions given from a safe position.
  2. Tells subject to stand straight, place feet together, and hold arms at sides.
  3. Tells subject not to begin test until instructed to do so and asks if subject understands.
  4. Tells subject to raise one leg, either leg, approximately 6″ from the ground, keeping raised foot parallel to the ground and gives demonstration.
  5. Tells subject to keep both legs straight and to look at elevated foot.
  6. Tells subject to count out loud in the following manner: one thousand one, one thousand two, one thousand three, and so on until told to stop, and gives demonstration.
  7. Asks subject if all instructions are understood.

Balance and counting phase

  1. Checks actual time subject holds leg up. (Time for 30 seconds.)
30sec · officer times it
The officer times the test, not the subject’s count. Discontinue after 30 seconds.
