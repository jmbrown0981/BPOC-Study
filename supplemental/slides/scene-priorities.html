Major steps officers take at ANY scene

  1. 1

    Assessment of threats

  2. 2

    Secure the scene

    • Neutralize threats
    • Establish the crime scene
    • Back off onlookers
  3. 3

    Render medical aid

  4. 4

    Conduct the investigation

  5. 5

    Reports / admin

Threats → Secure → Aid → Investigate → Report
