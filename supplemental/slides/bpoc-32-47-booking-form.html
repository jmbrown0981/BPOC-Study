Booking & Impound:
What Goes on the Form

Forms differ by department, but most ask for the same three groups of information. The arrest data is what gets the case through jail intake and into court, so it has to be complete and accurate.

Personal data 23

  • Name, nickname(s), aliases
  • Race, sex, age, date and place of birth
  • Height, weight, hair, eyes, complexion
  • Tattoos, scars, marks, deformities
  • Residence address, city and state
  • Driver’s license no. and state; SSN
  • Emergency contact name and address
  • Occupation, place of employment
  • Marital status and spouse’s name
  • Persons arrested with / known associates

Vehicle data 6

  • Color
  • Year of manufacture
  • Make
  • Body style
  • Registration: state and year; plate number and/or letters
  • Location of vehicle

Arrest data 13

  • Charge (with any warrant numbers)
  • Place, date, and time of arrest
  • Arresting officer(s); booking officer
  • Time booked into jail
  • Complainant name, address, business and residence phone
  • Facts of arrest
  • Witness names, addresses, phones
  • Amount of bond
  • Whether the Miranda warning was given
  • Phone calls granted, and to whom
  • Prisoner’s attorney
