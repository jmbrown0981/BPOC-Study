Ch. 40 · Emergency Medical Assistance · BPOC 40.5

Two Acronyms,
One Assessment

MARCH and XABCDE cover the same checks in the same order. The only difference: MARCH puts head injury and body temperature together under H; XABCDE splits them into D (Disability) and E (Environment).

MARCHXABCDEChecks
MMassive HemorrhageXeXsanguinationWound sweep: legs, neck, arms
AAirwayAAirwayOpen, clear, assess
RRespirationsBBreathingWound sweep: chest, abdomen, back · Monitor breathing: normal vs abnormal
CCirculationCCirculationPulse check: radial vs carotid · Skin check: normal vs abnormal
HHeadDDisabilityWound sweep: head, face, eyes, ears · Pupil check: dilated vs pinpoint vs unequal
HHypothermia / HyperthermiaEEnvironmentTemperature check: normal vs hot vs cold

MARCH — where you check

MMassive Hemorrhage→AAirway→RRespirations→CCirculation→HHead / Hypo / Hyperthermia
MMassive Hemorrhage
  • Wound sweep: legs, neck, arms
AAirway
  • Open, clear, assess the airway
RRespirations
  • Wound sweep: chest, abdomen, back
  • Monitor breathing: normal vs abnormal
CCirculation
  • Pulse check: radial vs carotid
  • Skin check: normal vs abnormal
HHead / Hypothermia / Hyperthermia
  • Wound sweep: head, face, eyes, ears
  • Pupil check: dilated vs pinpoint vs unequal
  • Temperature check: normal vs hot vs cold
Respirations includes the back: roll or reach under the casualty to sweep it.
