Say the Instructions — SFST Verbal Practice

Pick a test, press the mic, and give the instructions out loud from memory as if the subject were in front of you. You get two grades: key points (every instruction a clue depends on) and script match (how closely you followed the Participant Manual script). Companion to the SFST slides.

Why two grades? Following the script word for word is never wrong. But what holds up in court is that the subject was given every instruction a clue depends on, so there is no “the subject was never told to ___” defense. Key points should be 100%; script match shows how close to verbatim you were. Demonstrations (“like this”) are part of the script but can’t be graded by voice.
Speech recognition isn’t available in this browser. It works in Chrome, Edge, and Safari (desktop, Android, and iPhone). You can still practice by typing your instructions into the box and pressing Grade.
