How this differs from the Chapter 10 Alt Quiz case law bank: this guide is a standalone reference built from the course's dedicated case-law list, organized by legal topic instead of BPOC's chapter/LO structure. Some cases overlap with the Chapter 10 material; three are new here (Pennsylvania v. Mimms, Maryland v. Wilson, and the 2025 decision Barnes v. Felix). Where a case's citation is commonly mis-copied in study materials (Payton, Shatzer, Steagald), this guide flags the correct parallel citation rather than silently fixing it.
