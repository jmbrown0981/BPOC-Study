Statistics & Numbers Sorting Game

Chapter 29 is loaded with statistics — prevalence rates, use-of-force figures, PTSD and suicide data, IQ ranges, and diversion-program outcomes. Drag (or tap) each statistic onto the topic category it belongs to. A correct drop scores a point and locks the card in; a miss gets tracked and the card goes back in the tray so you can try again. New rounds pull a fresh, random mix from all fourteen categories. Note: several categories share similar-looking numbers on purpose (e.g., PTSD rates for the general population vs. veterans, or officer suicide vs. veteran suicide) — that overlap is exactly what makes sorting them a useful way to lock in which figure goes with which population.
