Symptom → Condition Sorting Game

Five conditions officers commonly encounter in the field — Traumatic Brain Injury, Dementia/Alzheimer's Disease, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Intellectual Disability, and PTSD — each get their own box. Drag (or tap) each card onto the condition it describes. Cards mix observable symptoms and behaviors with causes, risk factors, and the communication techniques the chapter recommends for that condition, since telling those apart in the field matters as much as spotting the symptom itself. Some cards look similar across conditions on purpose (e.g., confusion or agitation can show up in several boxes) — read for the specific behavior described, not just the general theme.
