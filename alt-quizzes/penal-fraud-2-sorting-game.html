Fraud II — Fiduciary, Identity & Real Property Fraud Sorting Game

The second of two Chapter 32 (Fraud) games. Eighteen boxes: misapplication of fiduciary property, fraudulent securing of document execution, fraudulent destruction/removal/concealment of a writing, simulating legal process, refusal to execute release of a fraudulent lien, deceptive marketing of an academic product, fraudulent use of identifying information, fraudulent/fictitious degrees, exploitation of a child/elderly/disabled individual, fraudulent military records, financial abuse of an elderly individual, and §32.56 — which the legislature has enacted four separate times under the same number for four unrelated offenses (gift card fraud, mail receptacle keys, fraudulent real-property conveyance documents, and AI-generated-media/phishing financial abuse), each kept here as its own box — plus fraudulent residential leasing, real property fraud, and malicious solicitation of a disaster victim. The cards are each section's first-level subsections.
