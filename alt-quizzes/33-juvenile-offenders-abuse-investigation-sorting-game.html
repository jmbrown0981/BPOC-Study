Child Abuse & Neglect Investigation Sorting Game

Eighteen boxes built entirely from Family Code Chapter 261 (Investigation of Report of Child Abuse or Neglect) plus the two related offenses/duties that sit alongside it: definitions, who must report and what must be reported (§§261.001, 261.101–261.104), how a report gets referred, immunities, and the penalties for a false report or a failure to report (§§261.105–261.109), Penal Code §38.17 (failure to stop or report a sexual or assaultive offense against a child), privileged communication, how an investigation is conducted and by whom — including CCP art. 2A.057 — colocation of investigators, interference with an investigation, and investigations inside juvenile justice programs and facilities (§§261.202–261.405). With 18 boxes the categories default to a collapsed view — expand them as you place cards, or size a round down with Categories/Cards-per-category.
