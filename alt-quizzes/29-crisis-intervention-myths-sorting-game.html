Mental Illness Myths vs. Reality Sorting Game

Each box is one of the seven common myths about mental illness the chapter dispels. Drag (or tap) the matching reality statement onto the myth it corrects. A correct drop scores a point and locks the card in — click it to see the supporting detail/citation; a miss gets tracked and the card goes back in the tray so you can try again. Since there are only seven myths total, a round can include anywhere from 2 up to all 7.
