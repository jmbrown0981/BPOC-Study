Deadlines & Numeric Thresholds Cheat Sheet

Chapter 33 is unusually deadline-heavy — every numeric time limit and age/quantity threshold across the chapter's statutes, in one searchable table, grouped by subject area.

← Study Hub
Custody & Detention Records & Statements Abuse/Neglect Reporting School Notification Missing Children Emergency Possession & CAC Numeric Thresholds
Two different kinds of numbers: the first six tables are true deadlines — a clock that starts running on an event. The last table is thresholds — ages, dollar amounts, and offense grades that don't involve a clock at all. Mixing the two up (e.g., treating an age cutoff like a deadline) is an easy way to miss a test question that's really asking which category a number belongs to.
