Theft Sorting Game

Twenty-three boxes, all of Chapter 31: theft, theft of service, theft of trade secrets, theft by check, unauthorized use of a vehicle, value and aggregation rules, tampering with identification numbers, multichannel video/information services offenses, organized retail theft, unauthorized acquisition of financial information, cargo theft, petroleum/oil-and-gas equipment theft, mail theft, unauthorized possession of a catalytic converter or copper/brass material, and real property theft. Boxes default to a collapsed view given the size. The cards are each section's first-level subsections.
