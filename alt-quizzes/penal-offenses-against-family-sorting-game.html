Fifteen boxes covering all of Chapter 25: bigamy, prohibited sexual conduct, interference with child custody, agreement to abduct from custody, enticing a child, criminal nonsupport, harboring a runaway child, violation of family-violence court orders/bond conditions (including bias/prejudice-motivated protective orders), sale or purchase of a child, unregulated custody transfer of an adopted child, advertising for placement of a child, interference with a guardian's rights, and continuous violence against the family.
