Disorderly Conduct I Sorting Game

The first of two Chapter 42 games — Chapter 42 alone runs 26 sections, so it's split in half. Thirteen boxes: disorderly conduct, riot, obstructing a highway or passageway, the speech/expression defense, disrupting a meeting or procession, funeral service disruptions, false alarm or report, false report to induce an emergency response, silent or abusive 9-1-1 calls, interference with an emergency request for assistance, harassment, stalking, and unlawful disclosure of a residence address or phone number. The cards are each section's first-level subsections.
