      Art. 36.01(b) — defense opens early

        Art. 36.01 does not list the court’s charge, closing arguments, or the verdict. Those come from other articles: the judge delivers the written charge before argument begins (Art. 36.14), the judge may regulate the order of argument but the State gets the concluding address (Art. 36.07), and the court may allow testimony at any time before argument concludes if needed for the due administration of justice (Art. 36.02).
