Drag (or tap) each substance onto the Penalty Group it belongs to under HSC Chapter 481. Note: marihuana isn't included here on purpose — it isn't in the Penalty Group scheme at all, and gets its own dedicated offense ladder (HSC 481.120/481.121); see the Penalty Groups & Offense Chart for that.
