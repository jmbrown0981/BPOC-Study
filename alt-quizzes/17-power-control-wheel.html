    The full wheel (reference)

    The outer rim of the wheel is Physical and Sexual Violence. The spokes are the tactics used alongside, or in place of, that violence. BPOC 17.13: the tactics can be subtle, continuous, and escalate over time, and they are used in combination with each other. The wheel says “her” to reflect the statistics when it was created; anybody can be a victim. Source: Domestic Abuse Intervention Programs, Duluth, MN (1984).
