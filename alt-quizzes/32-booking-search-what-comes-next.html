    Reference — LO 32.45 reasons and full search order

    Reasons for the search

    • Safety
    • Eliminate suicide risk and prevent death in custody (suicide screening for all prisoners being booked; see the Commission on Jail Standards)
    • Security of the facility
    • Safeguarding the prisoner’s personal property

    Who searches

    • Except in an emergency, a male officer searches a male prisoner and a female officer searches a female prisoner.
    • The search should be done out of view of the opposite sex.
    • Female searches are generally the same, plus the brassiere and, if she is wearing a dress, raising it to search the undergarment waistband.
    • Strip searches are regulated by the Texas Commission on Jail Standards.

      The BPOC list ends with “use other applicable techniques” before the female additions; it isn’t a distinct step, so the game leaves it out. Instructor note references: CCP Art. 49.18 (death in custody) and Penal Code 9.53 (maintaining security in a correctional facility).
