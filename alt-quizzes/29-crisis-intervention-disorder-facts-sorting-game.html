Fact → Disorder Sorting Game

The companion to the Disorder → Type game: here each box is one specific disorder (17 total across the five types), and the cards are the defining facts, symptoms, causes, effects, and associated substances the chapter attaches to it. Drag (or tap) each fact onto the disorder it describes. This is the biggest sorting game on the site — up to 17 boxes and 99 cards — so boxes default to a collapsed, pill-style view; expand them to see cards as you place them, or use Categories/Cards-per-category to size a round down. Some disorders naturally have more distinguishing facts than others (e.g., Dysthymia has just 2, Bipolar Disorder has 9) — that reflects how much space the chapter spends on each one, not a gap in the game.

How to play: either drag a card straight onto a disorder box, or tap/click a card to pick it up and then tap/click the box you think it belongs to. Right answer → the card locks into that box (green, with a checkmark — click it to see the extra detail). Wrong answer → the card shakes, the miss is counted, and it goes back to the tray so you can try a different box. Finish every card in the round to trigger the round-complete banner, then hit New Round for a fresh random deal.
