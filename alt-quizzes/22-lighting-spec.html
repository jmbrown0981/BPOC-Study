Vehicle Lighting — Height & Year Exceptions

Every mounting-height and count rule Transportation Code Chapter 547 sets for vehicle lamps and reflectors, checked against the federal standard (49 C.F.R. §571.108 / FMVSS 108) Texas incorporates by reference, with every model-year and manufacture-date exception flagged.

← Study Hub
How to read this: Texas Transportation Code §547.3215 makes the current (or, for an older vehicle, the then-current) federal standard in 49 C.F.R. §571.108 apply to lighting and reflective equipment unless Chapter 547 specifically says otherwise — and 37 TAC §21.2 adopts FMVSS 108 as the DPS equipment standard on the same terms. Where TC 547 states its own height or count, that number is what a Texas officer enforces directly, even where it differs from the federal figure. Federal figures shown are FMVSS 108's Table IV passenger-car requirements, included for comparison only — always cite the Texas statute for a BPOC exam answer.
