HSC 365.012 Illegal Dumping Value Ladder — graded by weight/volume, not dollars ▾

Same idea as the Theft & Criminal Mischief value ladder on the Penal Code Offense Reference page, except illegal dumping grades by weight or volume instead of dollar value — whichever measurement puts the conduct in the higher tier controls. Applies to disposing (a), receiving for disposal (b), or transporting for disposal (c) at a place that isn't an approved solid waste site.

Discarding lighted materials (Subsec. (a-1) — a lit match, cigarette, or cigar that starts a fire) is charged under this same section but has its own baseline: a misdemeanor punishable by a fine up to $500 and/or up to 30 days jail when the litter involved weighs under 500 lb or under 100 cu ft — it isn't formally labeled a "Class B misdemeanor" even though the numbers land in that neighborhood. Above that quantity, it escalates through the same Class A / state-jail-felony tiers above.

Repeat offenders (Subsec. (h)) move up one full category on a subsequent conviction under this section — a second Class C becomes a Class B, and so on.

Source: Health & Safety Code § 365.012. See also Codes & Sections Index for the related litter definitions (§§ 365.011(5), (6), (8)).
