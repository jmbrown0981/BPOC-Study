## LO 22.16: Authorized Emergency Vehicles (TC Ch.546)

### Q1 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.16 · **Source:** TC 546.001

In operating an authorized emergency vehicle, the operator may park or stand irrespective of another provision, proceed past a red or stop signal or stop sign after slowing as necessary for safe operation, exceed a maximum speed limit as long as the operator does not endanger life or property, and disregard a regulation governing the ______ of movement or turning in specified directions.

**Answer:** direction

**Explanation:** 546.001(4) is the fourth permissible-conduct category — disregarding direction-of-movement or turning regulations — alongside parking/standing, proceeding past signals, and exceeding speed limits.

### Q2 — Multiple Choice
