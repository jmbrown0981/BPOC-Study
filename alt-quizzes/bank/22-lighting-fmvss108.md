# Chapter 22: Traffic Code and Crash Investigations — Vehicle Lighting & FMVSS 108 (LO 22.17) — Question Bank

*Sourced from THP-68, the TxDPS Transportation Code Training Program's synopsis of the Federal Motor
Vehicle Safety Standard 108 (49 C.F.R. 571.108) lighting requirements and how they interact with
Transportation Code Chapter 547. Fill-in-the-blank and multiple choice questions are paired so each
fact is tested more than once.*

### Q2 — Concept
**LO:** 22.17 · **Source:** TC 547.3215

Under TC 547.3215, which vehicles must comply with the federal lighting standard (FMVSS 108)?

- [x] Any vehicle model year 1968 or newer, using the federal standard in effect at the time the vehicle
      was manufactured (or the current standard)
- [ ] Only commercial motor vehicles, regardless of model year
- [ ] Only vehicles manufactured after 2000
- [ ] All vehicles regardless of model year, with no pre-1968 exception

**Explanation:** TC 547.3215 adopts the federal lighting standard for any vehicle model year 1968 or
newer; a vehicle manufactured before January 1, 1968 is not required to conform to federal standards
and only needs to comply with TC Chapter 547.
