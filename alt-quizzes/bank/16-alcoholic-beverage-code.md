*Covers LOs 16.1-16.6 (definitions; duties of peace officers; search warrants and inspections; offenses and punishment;
hours of sale and consumption; criminal vs. administrative actions). Code questions are checked against the site's copy of

## LO 16.1 — Definitions (Alcoholic Beverage Code §§1.04, 1.05, 11.49, 106.01)

### Q1 — Recall
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(1)

Under the Alcoholic Beverage Code, a beverage is an "alcoholic beverage" if it contains more than what amount of alcohol by volume and is capable of use for beverage purposes?

- [x] More than one-half of one percent
- [ ] More than 1 percent
- [ ] More than 3.2 percent
- [ ] More than 5 percent

**Explanation:** §1.04(1): alcohol, or any beverage containing more than one-half of one percent of alcohol by volume, capable of use for beverage purposes either alone or when diluted.

---

### Q2 — Concept
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(1), (15)

How do the alcohol thresholds in the definitions of "alcoholic beverage" and "malt beverage" differ?

- [ ] They are identical: both require at least 5 percent
- [ ] An alcoholic beverage requires more than 5 percent; a malt beverage requires 4 percent or more
- [x] An alcoholic beverage contains MORE THAN one-half of one percent; a malt beverage contains one-half of one percent OR MORE
- [ ] A malt beverage has no minimum alcohol content

**Explanation:** §1.04(1) says "more than one-half of one percent"; §1.04(15) says "one-half of one percent or more." The wording differs by the boundary value of exactly 0.5 percent.

---

### Q3 — Recall
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(3)

Spirit coolers fall under the definition of "distilled spirits" even if their alcohol content is as low as what?

- [ ] One-half of one percent by volume
- [ ] Five percent by volume
- [ ] Ten percent by volume
- [x] Four percent alcohol by volume

**Explanation:** §1.04(3): distilled spirits include spirit coolers that may have an alcoholic content as low as four percent alcohol by volume.

### Q4 — Recall

Under §1.04(5), "liquor" is any alcoholic beverage, other than a malt beverage, containing alcohol in excess of what percentage by volume?

- [ ] One-half of one percent
- [ ] Four percent
- [x] Five percent
- [ ] Twelve percent

**Explanation:** §1.04(5): liquor is any alcoholic beverage, other than a malt beverage, containing alcohol in excess of five percent by volume, unless otherwise indicated.

### Q5 — Concept
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(15)

Which ingredient is the defining feature of a "malt beverage"?

- [ ] It is made from fermented grape juice
- [ ] It is mixed and served by the drink on a mixed beverage permittee's premises

**Explanation:** §1.04(15). Distillation describes distilled spirits (§1.04(3)); fermented fruit juice describes wine (§1.04(7)).

### Q6 — Concept
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(13)

A "mixed beverage" is a beverage composed in whole or part of an alcoholic beverage, served for consumption where?

- [ ] Anywhere, as long as the container is sealed
- [ ] Only off the premises, in a sealed container
- [x] On the premises where it is served or sold by the holder of a mixed beverage permit (or certain other permits or certificates)

**Explanation:** §1.04(13): one or more servings, in a sealed or unsealed container of any legal size, for consumption on the premises where served or sold by a mixed beverage permittee, certain nonprofit temporary event permittees, a private club registration permittee, or certain retailer late hours certificate holders.

### Q7 — Classification
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(4)

Which of the following is an "illicit beverage" under §1.04(4)?

### Q9 — Concept
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(11), (16)

A bartender works for a bar that holds a mixed beverage permit. Is the bartender a "permittee" under the Alcoholic Beverage Code?

### Q10 — Concept

### Q17 — Recall
**LO:** 16.1 · **Source:** Alcoholic Beverage Code §1.04(28); Penal Code §6.03(d)
