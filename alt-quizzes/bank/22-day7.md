## LO 22.18: Electric Personal Assistive Mobility Devices & Neighborhood Electric Vehicles (TC 551.201-.304)

### Q1 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.18 · **Source:** TC 551.201

Under TC 551.201, an "electric personal assistive mobility device" is defined as a two ______ wheeled device designed for transporting one person that is ______.

**Answer:** non-tandem | self-balancing

**Explanation:** EPAMDs (Segway-type devices) are defined by two non-tandem wheels and a self-balancing design, propelled by an electric propulsion system.

---

### Q2 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.18 · **Source:** TC 551.201

- [x] It is self-balancing

- [ ] It is designed to carry two people
- [ ] It is propelled solely by human power

**Explanation:** The device must be self-balancing with two non-tandem wheels, propelled by an electric propulsion system — not human power.
