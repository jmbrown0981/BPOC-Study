## LO 30.3 - Describe a Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI).

### Q7 — Definition
**LO:** 30.3 · **Source:** BPOC 30.3

- [x] A blow to the head or a penetrating head injury
- [ ] Lack of oxygen to the brain or exposure to toxins
- [ ] A stroke or a brain tumor
- [ ] Drug use or an infection like meningitis

**Explanation:** A TBI is a type of acquired brain injury that disrupts normal brain function and is caused by a blow to the head or a penetrating head injury.

---

### Q8 — Definition
**LO:** 30.3 · **Source:** BPOC 30.3

- [ ] An injury where an object penetrates the skull and enters the brain
- [ ] An injury caused entirely by an internal event, not an external force
- [ ] An injury that always results in a loss of consciousness

**Explanation:** A closed-head injury is when the skull stays intact, as opposed to an open-head injury, where an object penetrates the skull and enters the brain.

---

### Q9 — Definition
**LO:** 30.3 · **Source:** BPOC 30.3

What is an "open-head injury," as distinguished in this chapter?

- [x] An injury where an object penetrates the skull and enters the brain
- [ ] An injury where the skull stays intact
- [ ] An injury caused by an internal event rather than an external force
- [ ] An injury that always results in permanent impairment

**Explanation:** An open-head injury is when an object penetrates the skull and enters the brain, as opposed to a closed-head injury, where the skull stays intact.

---

### Q10 — Scenario
