## LO 1.1 — Concept of professionalism

### Q1 — Definition
**LO:** 1.1 · **Source:** BPOC 1.1 (Oxford English Dictionary)

Per the Oxford English Dictionary definition presented in this chapter, a "profession" is a vocation in which professed knowledge of some branch of learning is used how?

- [x] In its application to the affairs of others, or in the practice of an art based upon it
- [ ] Solely for personal financial gain within a regulated trade
- [ ] Exclusively within the three learned professions of divinity, law, and medicine

**Explanation:** The OED defines profession as "the occupation which one professes to be skilled in and to follow... in which professed knowledge of some branch of learning is used in its application to the affairs of others, or in the practice of an art based upon it." The three learned professions (divinity, law, medicine) are mentioned as a specific application of that definition, not the definition itself.
