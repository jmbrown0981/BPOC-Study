### Q29 — Scenario
**LO:** 4.4 · **Source:** §217.7(a)(7), §217.7(a)(10)(A)

An officer resigned from a Texas department six months ago and is starting a new appointment.
The new department already has his L1 ready to sign, his F5-R completed, a full background
investigation, his Personal History Statement, and his CCH and DD-214 on file. Which of the
following is he still missing before he can be appointed?

- [ ] A copy of his DD-214
- [x] Current firearms qualification within the last 12 months, plus new L2 and L3 forms (since more than 180 days have passed since his last appointment)
- [ ] A new Personal History Statement

**Explanation:** §217.7(a)(7) requires proof of weapons qualification within the preceding 12
months for peace officers, and §217.7(a)(10)(A) requires a fresh L2 and L3 for any licensee
whose last appointment was more than 180 days ago — six months clears that threshold.
