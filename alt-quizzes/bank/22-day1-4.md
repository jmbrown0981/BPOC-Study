## Day 1 — LO 22.1: Vehicle Types (TC 541.201)

### Q1 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(1)

Which of the following, by itself, is enough to make a vehicle an "authorized emergency vehicle" under TC 541.201(1)?

- [ ] Any vehicle displaying a red or blue flashing light
- [x] A private vehicle of a volunteer firefighter responding to a fire alarm
- [ ] Any tow truck responding to a highway collision
- [ ] A personal vehicle equipped with a siren

**Explanation:** 541.201(1) enumerates specific categories (fire/police vehicles, licensed ambulances, certain EMS vehicles, designated municipal/county vehicles, volunteer firefighter/EMS private vehicles while responding, industrial emergency response vehicles, blood/tissue bank vehicles on emergency runs, federal law enforcement vehicles) — simply having warning lights or a siren does not qualify a vehicle.

### Q2 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(2)

Under TC 541.201(2), a "______" is a device, excluding a moped, capable of being ridden solely using human power that has two tandem wheels at least one of which is more than ______ inches in diameter (or three wheels meeting the same threshold, or any number of wheels with adaptive technology for a rider with a disability).

**Answer:** Bicycle | 14

**Explanation:** The 14-inch wheel-diameter threshold is what separates a "bicycle" from a small-wheeled tricycle or similar toy under this definition.

### Q3 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(3)

Under TC 541.201(3), which of the following independently qualifies as a "bus"?

- [x] A motor vehicle, other than a taxicab, designed and used to transport persons for compensation
- [ ] Any vehicle owned by a school district
- [ ] A motor vehicle designed to accommodate exactly 8 passengers
- [ ] A vehicle used to transport cargo for compensation

**Explanation:** "Bus" has two independent prongs: capacity (designed for more than 10 passengers, including the operator) OR for-compensation passenger transport other than a taxicab — either one qualifies.

### Q4 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(4)

"______" means a motor vehicle designed and used primarily as a farm implement to draw an implement of husbandry, including a plow or a mowing machine.

**Answer:** Farm Tractor

**Explanation:** A farm tractor is the vehicle that draws an implement of husbandry — the implement itself is defined separately under 541.201(6).

### Q5 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(5)

TC 541.201(5) defines "house trailer" as a trailer or semitrailer (other than a towable recreational vehicle) built on a permanent chassis with plumbing/heating/electrical systems that is also which of the following, while in traveling mode?

- [ ] Less than 20 feet in length, excluding the tow bar

### Q6 — Fill-in-Blank

**Explanation:** "Implement of husbandry" and "farm tractor" are separate, cross-referencing definitions in 541.201(4) and (6) — the tractor draws the implement, not the other way around.

### Q8 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(8)

A "moped" cannot attain a speed of more than ______ miles per hour, cannot produce more than five-brake horsepower, and if it has an internal combustion engine, has a piston displacement of ______ cubic centimeters or less with no required gear shifting.

### Q9 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(8), (9)

An operator's saddle-equipped vehicle with no more than three wheels when propelled tops out at 45 mph and has an internal combustion engine with a piston displacement of 90 cubic centimeters. Under TC 541.201, this vehicle is legally a:

- [x] Motorcycle
- [ ] Moped
- [ ] Farm tractor
- [ ] Special mobile equipment

**Explanation:** It exceeds the moped's 30 mph speed cap and 50cc displacement limit, so by process of elimination it falls under "motorcycle" — a motor vehicle (other than a tractor or moped) with a rider's saddle and no more than three wheels when propelled.

### Q10 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(11)

"______" means a self-propelled vehicle or a vehicle that is propelled by electric power from overhead trolley wires. The term does not include an electric bicycle or an electric personal assistive mobility device.

**Answer:** Motor Vehicle

### Q11 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(11-a), (15), (16)

A bus used to transport secondary students on a school-related activity trip (not on a route to/from school) that was manufactured to federal school-bus safety standards, except for the standards requiring alternately flashing red lights and movable stop arms, is a:

- [x] Multifunction school activity bus
- [ ] School bus
- [ ] School activity bus
- [ ] Passenger car

**Explanation:** A "school bus" (541.201(16)) must meet all federal school-bus safety standards including flashing lights and stop arms; a "school activity bus" (541.201(15)) is a bus designed for more than 15 passengers owned/operated by a school district that is not built to those federal standards at all. The "multifunction school activity bus" (541.201(11-a)) is the one built to most, but not all, of the federal standards.

### Q12 — Fill-in-Blank

"______" means a motor vehicle, other than a motorcycle, used to transport persons and designed to accommodate ______ or fewer passengers, including the operator.

**Answer:** Passenger Car | 10

**Explanation:** The 10-passenger-or-fewer threshold in 541.201(12) is the mirror image of the "bus" definition's more-than-10-passenger threshold in 541.201(3).

### Q13 — Multiple Choice

A vehicle without motive power, designed to be drawn by another vehicle and secured to it by a pole, reach, or boom, and ordinarily used to transport a long or irregularly shaped load like pipes or structural members, is a:

### Q14 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(18)

"______" means a vehicle that is not designed or used primarily to transport persons or property and that is only incidentally operated on a highway, including ditchdigging apparatus, well boring apparatus, and road construction and maintenance machinery.

**Answer:** Special Mobile Equipment

**Explanation:** The definition specifically excludes a vehicle designed to transport persons or property that merely has machinery attached (like a house trailer, dump truck, or truck-mounted crane) — those remain their own vehicle type.

### Q15 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(23)

TC 541.201(23) defines "vehicle" as a device that can be used to transport or draw persons or property on a highway. Which of the following is expressly excluded from that definition?

- [x] A device exclusively used on stationary rails or tracks
- [ ] A motorcycle
- [ ] A trailer
- [ ] Special mobile equipment

**Explanation:** 541.201(23) excludes only (A) a device exclusively used on stationary rails or tracks and (B) manufactured housing as defined by the Occupations Code — everything else that transports or draws persons/property on a highway qualifies as a "vehicle."

### Q223 — Fill-in-Blank
**LO:** 22.15 · **Source:** TC 545.454(a)

When an automated driving system installed on a motor vehicle is engaged, the automated driving system is the ______ of the vehicle, including for purposes of assessing compliance with applicable traffic or motor vehicle laws.

**Answer:** operator

**Explanation:** Under 545.454(c), neither a licensed human driver nor a license issued under Chapter 521 or 522 is required to operate an automated motor vehicle while its automated driving system is engaged — but 545.454(b) still directs any citation for a traffic violation to the vehicle's owner or authorization holder.

### Q224 — Multiple Choice
**LO:** 22.15 · **Source:** TC 545.455(b)(4)

Under TC 545.455(b), an automated motor vehicle may not operate on a highway or street with its automated driving system engaged unless, among other requirements, it is capable of achieving a:

- [x] Minimal risk condition if the automated driving system fails
- [ ] Top speed of at least 75 miles per hour
- [ ] Full stop within 50 feet at highway speed
- [ ] Remote shutdown within 10 seconds

**Explanation:** "Minimal risk condition" is defined in 545.451(11) as a stable and stopped condition the vehicle can reach to reduce collision risk when a trip cannot or should not continue — one of six conditions 545.455(b) requires before an automated motor vehicle may legally operate with the system engaged.
