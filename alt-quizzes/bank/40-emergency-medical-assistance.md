## LO 40.1 — Legal Aspects of Emergency Medical Assistance

### Q1 — True/False
**LO:** 40.1 · **Source:** Civil Practice and Remedies Code Sec. 74.152

True or False: Persons not licensed or certified in the healing arts who in good faith administer emergency care as emergency medical service personnel **are** liable in civil damages for an act performed in administering the care unless the act is wilfully or wantonly negligent. This section applies without regard to whether the care is provided for or in expectation of remuneration.

- [x] False
- [ ] True

**Explanation:** These persons are not liable in civil damages, per Civil Practice and Remedies Code Sec. 74.152 (AKA "The Good Samaritan Law")

### Q2 — Recall
**LO:** 40.1 · **Source:** CCP 2B.0301

A peace officer who encounters an injured person while discharging the officer's official duties shall immediately and as necessary:

- [x] Request emergency medical services personnel to provide the person with emergency medical services and while waiting for emergency medical services personnel to arrive, provide first aid or treatment to the person to the extent of the officer's skill and training
- [ ] Just report the incident to emergency medical services
- [ ] Ask a supervisor the correct course of action
- [ ] Perform CPR immediately

**Explanation:** CCP Art. 2B.0301(a) requires the officer to immediately and as necessary request EMS and, while waiting, provide first aid or treatment to the extent of the officer's skill and training.

### Q3 — Recall
**LO:** 40.1 · **Source:** CCP 2B.0301

The peace officer is not required to request emergency medical services or provide first aid or treatment under Subsection (a) of Sec. 2B.0301. DUTY TO REQUEST AND RENDER AID of the CCP if:

- [ ] It is 15 minutes or less until the end of his shift
- [ ] The injured party is not a citizen of the state
- [x] Making the request or providing the treatment would expose the officer or another person to a risk of bodily injury or the officer is injured and physically unable to make the request or provide the treatment
- [ ] The injured party has previously been convicted of a felony

**Explanation:** CCP Art. 2B.0301(b): the duty does not apply if making the request or providing the treatment would expose the officer or another person to a risk of bodily injury, or if the officer is injured and physically unable to do it.

### Q4 — Recall
**LO:** 40.1 · **Source:** Transportation Code Sec. 550.023. DUTY TO GIVE INFORMATION AND RENDER AID

A Peace officer's duties include which of the below, per Sec. 550.023. DUTY TO GIVE INFORMATION AND RENDER AID of the Transportation Code?

- [x] Provide any person injured in the collision reasonable assistance, including transporting or making arrangements for transporting the person to a physician or hospital for medical treatment if it is apparent that treatment is necessary, or if the injured person requests the transportation
- [ ] Immediately begin work on a crash report
- [ ] Ask a supervisor the correct course of action
- [ ] Leave the scene to allow emergency medical services ample room to assist

**Explanation:** Transportation Code Sec. 550.023 requires reasonable assistance to any person injured in the collision, including transporting or arranging transport to a physician or hospital when treatment is apparently necessary or the injured person requests it.
