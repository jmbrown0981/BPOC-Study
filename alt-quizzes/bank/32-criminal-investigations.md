*Covers BPOC Chapter 32 (LOs 32.1–32.63). Every 32.1 definition and every 32.6 investigator
characteristic has both a multiple-choice question and a typed Term ID question. List-based LOs
use a "pick the category" format where the answer choices are the list headings — property
offender types (32.7), auto theft categories, homicide categories, search methods, photo vantage
points, measurement methods, evidence categories, examination phases, witnessmanship principles,
and defense tactics. Includes the BPOC search-method scenarios and the chapter-resource mock
investigation scenarios.*
