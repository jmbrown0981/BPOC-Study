## LO 17.2 — Define Consent in the Context of Sexual Assault

### Q9 — Term ID
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** Penal Code Sec. 1.07

**Answer:** consent

__________ means assent in fact, whether express or apparent.

**Explanation:** Penal Code Sec. 1.07(a)(11): "Consent" means assent in fact, whether express or apparent.

---

### Q10 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** BPOC LO 17.2

Two married adults agree to engage in sexual intercourse in the privacy of their bedroom.

- [x] Consent
- [ ] Not Consent

**Explanation:** Both parties are adults who assent in fact (Penal Code Sec. 1.07). Nothing in the scenario involves coercion, force, threats, intoxication, or inability to resist.

---

### Q11 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** Penal Code Sec. 22.011

A 15 year old female and a 16 year old male boyfriend/girlfriend, both underage, agree to have sex.

- [x] Consent
- [ ] Not Consent

**Explanation:** While both are considered children and underage, they are protected by the affirmative defense that they are less than 3 years apart, over the age of 14, not prohibited from being married, and not sex offenders. This is also known as the "Romeo and Juliet" defense.

---

### Q12 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** BPOC LO 17.2

A male uses threats of violence to compel a female to commit a sexual act with him.

- [ ] Consent
- [x] Not Consent

**Explanation:** BPOC 17.2: it is not consent if a sexual act is accomplished at any time through coercion, physical force, violence, intoxication tactics, or even threats of violence.

---

### Q13 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** BPOC LO 17.2

While kissing, a male aggressively undresses the female. The female appears taken aback, but remains silent. The male penetrates the female; the female never says no, but appears in distress throughout the episode.

- [ ] Consent
- [x] Not Consent

**Explanation:** The victim remaining silent does not imply consent. The penal code does not state that one of the parties must say "no," or any other term or phrase to indicate they do not consent. In this scenario, it is possible the female party was in "frozen fright," the male party should have been aware that there was not consent, and this is therefore a sexual assault.

---

### Q14 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** BPOC LO 17.2

A female finds a male passed out drunk at a party. While he is unconscious, she performs sexual acts with him.

- [ ] Consent
- [x] Not Consent

**Explanation:** A person who is intoxicated and/or unconscious cannot legally consent. In this case, the victim is also unable to resist due to being unconscious, and is therefore a victim of sexual assault.

---

### Q15 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** BPOC LO 17.2

At a bar, a male keeps ordering drinks for a female and encouraging her to finish them. Once she is too intoxicated to be aware of what is happening, he takes her home and has sex with her.

- [ ] Consent
- [x] Not Consent

**Explanation:** BPOC 17.2: it is not consent if the act is accomplished through intoxication tactics, or if a person is intoxicated and unaware that the act is occurring.

---

### Q16 — Scenario

- [ ] Consent
- [x] Not Consent

**Explanation:** BPOC 17.2: it is not consent if the offender knows the victim has a mental disturbance or diminished mental capacity.

---

### Q17 — Scenario
**LO:** 17.2 · **Source:** BPOC LO 17.2

Two sober adults who have been dating for several months discuss and agree to have sex, and both remain willing participants throughout.

- [x] Consent

**Explanation:** Assent in fact, whether express or apparent (Penal Code Sec. 1.07), with no coercion, force, threats, intoxication, or inability to resist.

### Q38 — Recall
**LO:** 17.5 · **Source:** BPOC 17.5

- [x] Do not interrupt
- [ ] Ensure the investigative interview is conducted as soon as possible after the initial statement
