*This bank covers BPOC Chapter 22 learning objectives 22.1 through 22.11 — the Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3 material from the class's own day-by-day outline. Day 1 covers TC Chapter 541 vehicle/person/traffic definitions; Day 2 covers Chapter 541 miscellaneous terms, Health and Safety Code litter/dumping law, Chapter 542 obedience/offenses/penalties, and the Chapter 521/522 driver's-license and CDL scheme; Day 3 covers Chapter 544 signs/signals/markings and the Chapter 545 Subchapters B, C, and D driving rules (passing, turning/signaling, and right-of-way). ## Day 1 — LO 22.1: Vehicle Definitions (TC 541.201)

### Q1 — Scenario
**LO:** 22.1 · **Source:** TC 541.201(1)(G)

Which of the following private vehicles qualifies as an "authorized emergency vehicle" under TC 541.201?

- [x] The private vehicle of a volunteer firefighter or certified EMS employee/volunteer, while responding to a fire alarm or medical emergency
- [ ] Any private vehicle equipped with a siren and flashing lights
- [ ] The private vehicle of an off-duty municipal police officer running personal errands
- [ ] Any tow truck responding to a disabled-vehicle call

**Explanation:** TC 541.201(1)(G) includes a private vehicle of a volunteer firefighter or certified EMS employee/volunteer when responding to a fire alarm or medical emergency; merely having emergency-style equipment doesn't qualify a vehicle on its own.
