# Chapter 39: Canine Encounters — Question Bank

### Q1 — Term ID
**LO:** 39.1 · **Source:** BPOC 39.1; Penal Code §9.01(3)

**Answer:** Deadly Force

"______" means force that is intended or known by the actor to cause, or in the manner of its use or intended use is capable of causing, death or serious bodily injury.

**Explanation:** Penal Code §9.01(3) defines deadly force by its intended or known result OR by its capability — force that, in the manner of its use or intended use, is capable of causing death or serious bodily injury.

### Q2 — Term ID
**LO:** 39.1 · **Source:** BPOC 39.1; Penal Code §9.05

**Answer:** Reckless Injury of Innocent Third Person | Reckless Injury of an Innocent Third Person | Reckless Injury of Innocent Third Persons

Under the Penal Code Chapter 9 rule known as "______," even though an actor is justified in threatening or using force or deadly force against another, if in doing so they also recklessly injure or kill an innocent third person, the justification is unavailable in a prosecution for the reckless injury or killing of that innocent third person.

**Explanation:** Penal Code §9.05. Justification against the target does not carry over to a bystander you recklessly injure or kill — relevant any time an officer uses force (including against a dog) near other people.

---

### Q3 — Term ID
**LO:** 39.1 · **Source:** BPOC 39.1; Penal Code §9.06

**Answer:** Civil Remedies Unaffected

Under the Penal Code Chapter 9 rule known as "______," the fact that conduct is justified under Penal Code Chapter 9 does not abolish or impair any remedy for the conduct that is available in a civil suit.

**Explanation:** Penal Code §9.06. Being criminally justified does not shield the actor from a civil lawsuit.
